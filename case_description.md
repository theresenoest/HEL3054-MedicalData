# Case description

A population-based health study recruited participants between 2005 and 2008.
Baseline information includes demographics, smoking, physical activity, BMI,
blood pressure, cholesterol, diabetes, and education.

The cohort has been linked to several fictional national and hospital sources:

- patient registry;
- procedure register;
- prescription database;
- hospital electronic medical records;
- structured medical-record review;
- death and follow-up file.

A registry-based case-identification algorithm identifies participants with
ICD-10 codes in I20–I25, covering angina, acute ischaemic heart disease, and
chronic ischaemic heart disease.

The research team asks:

> Are people with registry IHD codes truly people with clinically documented IHD?

Students must distinguish:

- first observed registry code;
- first registry-recorded IHD event;
- first clinically documented IHD event;
- incident IHD after cohort baseline;
- prevalent IHD that existed before cohort baseline.

The files contain deliberately constructed examples of:

- outpatient angina with IHD not confirmed;
- repeated outpatient angina supporting IHD;
- historical MI documented years later;
- secondary IHD during admission for pneumonia;
- hospital transfer;
- invalid or incomplete dates;
- ICD-9 and ICD-10 coding;
- SNOMED concepts;
- procedures supporting IHD;
- clinically documented IHD without a registry diagnosis;
- death before administrative end of follow-up.
