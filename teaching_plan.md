# Teaching plan

## 09.15–10.00 — What does an IHD diagnosis mean in different data sources?

Students compare ICD codes, SNOMED concepts, procedures, prescriptions, and
clinical note excerpts.

Main outputs:

- data-source map;
- unit of observation for each file;
- broad, strict, and chart-review IHD definitions.

## 10.15–11.00 — Time is part of the phenotype

Students parse inconsistent dates, identify erroneous dates, construct patient
timelines, and define an index date for a first IHD event.

Main outputs:

- patient timeline;
- first-event rule;
- date rule;
- reflection on prevalent versus incident IHD.

## 11.15–12.00 — Registry validation study

Students compare registry phenotype algorithms with structured chart review.

Main outputs:

- two-by-two table;
- PPV;
- sensitivity, specificity, and NPV where possible;
- interpretation by hospital, setting, and calendar period.

## 13.15–14.00 — Building an analysis-ready first-IHD-event dataset

Students join cohort, contact, diagnosis, procedure, and follow-up data.

Main outputs:

- diagnosis-level linked dataset;
- person-level analysis-ready dataset;
- documented first-event algorithm.

## 14.15–15.00 — Phenotype clinic

Students compare broad and strict phenotype definitions, investigate discordant
cases, conduct simple sensitivity analyses, and complete a phenotype protocol.

Main outputs:

- phenotype protocol;
- sensitivity-analysis table;
- key limitations and data-quality reflection.
